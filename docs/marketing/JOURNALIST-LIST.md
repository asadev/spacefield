## Tracking

For each row, record in a separate sheet (not in this doc, since it
holds PII once filled):

- Date emailed
- Date confirmed receipt
- Embargo honored / lifted early / unknown
- Coverage published date + URL
- Tone (positive / neutral / critical)

Post-launch we use this to know who to nurture into a long-term
relationship.
